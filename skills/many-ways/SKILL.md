---
name: many-ways
description: Lay out four or five genuinely different ways to solve a problem, with trade-offs and a recommendation, before committing to one.
argument-hint: The problem or idea to explore
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Open the option space before we commit to an approach. Stop once the options are on the table: choosing and refining belong to the grilling that follows.

1. State the problem in one sentence. Look up facts in the code and docs yourself. Ask me only for a fact that changes which options exist, and ask once.
2. Produce four or five approaches that differ in kind, not in degree. Two variants of one idea count as one approach. Force the spread with whichever lenses fit:
   - **Opposite**: do the reverse of the obvious approach.
   - **No constraint**: drop the constraint that shapes the obvious approach.
   - **Ten times simpler**: the smallest version that still solves the real problem.
   - **Other owner**: put it in a different layer, package, or system.
   - **Not now**: do nothing, or defer, and say what that costs.
3. For each approach, give one line on what it is, what it gets us, what it costs, and what would make it fail.
4. Recommend one. Say why, and name the assumption the recommendation rests on.
5. End by asking me to pick, then suggest `/grill-me` on the chosen approach.
